Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Akash NextGen IT ("we", "us") is a software company. This page explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Details you send us through the contact form: your name, email address, subject and message.
  • Details you share when you message or call us on WhatsApp, email or LinkedIn.
  • Basic technical data such as browser type and pages visited, which our hosting provider may log automatically.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a quote.
  • To deliver and support projects you hire us for.
  • To keep this website secure and working properly.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with anyone except the service providers needed to run this website and respond to you.

Services this website uses

This website is hosted on Vercel. The contact form sends your message to our server, which is hosted on Render and stores it in a MongoDB database. The page also loads fonts from Google Fonts, a map from Google Maps and images from Unsplash. These providers may set their own cookies or collect technical data under their own privacy policies. We do not use advertising cookies.

How long we keep your information

We keep enquiries for as long as needed to respond to you and to manage any project we do together. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing akashnextgenit@gmail.com.
